# Ojaas portfolio — trial brief

## User and context
Ojaas Hampiholi's primary identity website, aimed at recruiters, founders, software engineers, data scientists, and AI engineers.

## Outcomes
Short term: AI engineering job opportunities and founder collaborations / consulting.
Long term: technical credibility and readership through a native blog and references to writing published elsewhere.

## Primary journey
Understand Ojaas's focus → inspect evidence in a project or technical article → follow source code / supporting writing → make contact.

## Scope
Home, project evidence, professional background, native blog, and cross-references to external writing. Responsive, accessible, HTML-first delivery with search metadata and machine-readable identity.
No invented employment, project results, testimonials, metrics, or articles attributed to Ojaas.

## Open inputs
Professional source material, approved public contact details, domain, existing writing, and preferred authoring workflow.

## Acceptance criteria
- Main content and navigation readable without client JavaScript.
- Projects distinguish verified facts from missing content.
- Native posts have stable URLs, author, publication date, summary, and references.
- External writing is clearly identified and linked to its original publication.
- Wide and narrow layouts, keyboard navigation, focus, and reduced motion verified in a browser.
- Build, links, metadata, structured data, sitemap, feed, and crawler directives checked.
- Trial assumptions and unverified checks recorded explicitly.
